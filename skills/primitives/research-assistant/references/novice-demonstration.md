# Novice use demonstrations and acceptance

Read for a beginner tutorial, an end-to-end assistant acceptance test, or a new research-case demonstration. Use the actual requested case and existing authorisation. A request to improve this skill does not authorise editing a current manuscript, installing globally, or starting a different study.

## Evidence before storytelling

Pin the intended project and verify that the host discovers the installed skill there. Record actual skill selection/loading or explicit invocation and its real response. Reading the skill as an ordinary file is a different event. Static link/format checks establish packaging only; they do not prove host discovery, selection, execution or helpful behaviour.

For each research stage, retain the real beginner question, assistant response, guidance/recommendation/delegated choice and reason, any required explicit workflow invocation, input/output artifacts, observed errors and verification. Maintain these in the existing operation evidence, not a second research registry. Keep proposed, attempted, executed and verified work distinct. Do not invent a missing call or result to bridge a sequence. If the host/tool is unavailable, identify the exact unmet step and keep end-to-end acceptance incomplete.

Use a newly agreed case when requested, with genuine evidence/data and an accepted design before estimation. Cover topic comparison, literature and contribution, hypotheses, data/measurement, method choice, diagnostics and corrections, chapter writing, citation checks, limitations and defence questions as appropriate to the accepted scope. Do not guarantee novelty, significance or a degree. Preserve unsuccessful tests and explain why the next action follows.

## What the learner sees

For screenshot-based teaching material, follow [Capture for tutorials](../../render-research-deliverable/references/html-reading-views.md#capture-for-tutorials). Retain the actual original image, capture time and corresponding action before making cropped/zoomed/annotated copies. Prefer formally available, authorised full-desktop captures when appropriate; otherwise use and identify the available real browser capture. Do not infer app-control authority from screenshot capability, promise unverified desktop access, or fabricate links/buttons/operations during editing.

Explain how to start in the correct project, call the assistant, ask for guidance/advice or bounded decisions, explicitly enter the appropriate skill when required, inspect its document/result, ask about an error, and resume in another conversation. Show authentic operation screenshots and readable document views. Use animation to clarify a research choice or a causal/statistical distinction; it cannot substitute for an actual skill call or real finding.

Use narration, captions and visual emphasis in the learner's language. Keep the learner-facing content free of program identifiers, command lines, filesystem paths, hashes, execution receipts, workpackage jargon and raw diagnostics; skill names are the exception. Show human-readable document titles and explain research methods accurately. Crop or redact technical/private regions of screenshots without hiding failures or altering the substantive response. Keep the full technical evidence separately accessible to the reviewer.

A designed student role must be labelled as teaching role-play, not a real participant. Label any reenactment where it occurs and never present it as native operation. For an actual-use acceptance film, reenactment, generated subtitles or an animated conversation cannot replace the real selection/response/artifact evidence. A shortened wait is acceptable; deleting the reasoning, a failed check or an authorisation boundary is not.

## Acceptance

Verify the actual stage sequence against the saved evidence before editing a success narrative. Then inspect screenshot/document readability, narration/caption synchronisation, chapter coverage and the honesty of every execution claim. A rendered movie is a media artifact; it does not by itself complete the assistant-use test. Report separately what was demonstrated, what was only explained, and every blocked or untested stage. Do not expose internal diagnostics in the academic body or the beginner tutorial to compensate for missing real-use evidence.

# Admit a degree-thesis topic on substance and title together

Apply this checklist to a requested master's or doctoral topic. Adapt the contribution standard to the degree, discipline and known institutional requirements. No title or cover layout is universally mandatory when the institution has not been specified. Do not promise degree approval.

## Keep three distinct objects

1. **Plain-language puzzle:** the learner's original observation or question, preserved verbatim when supplied. It starts inquiry; it is not yet a thesis topic.
2. **Research question:** the bounded relationship, explanation, interpretation, prediction or decision to investigate; identify the unit, population/setting and intended inference.
3. **Academic working title:** a concise scholarly description of that supported question. For a quantitative business/finance study, make the core constructs or variable relationship and the research population or scope clear, in the title/subtitle as appropriate. Do not force an unverified period, method, causality, interaction or novelty into it. The title is revisable until accepted, and acceptance does not validate its factual premises.

For example, “Why is cash tight when sales grow?” is a puzzle. Giving it a noun-heavy title does not establish a mechanism, a literature gap, a measurable liquidity construct or a feasible sample. A naming pattern such as “〈construct A and construct B: population/scope〉” is only a pattern, never a supplied topic or an accepted hypothesis.

## Evidence before admission

For each serious candidate, retain a compact comparison with:

- a concrete managerial/financial decision problem and audience;
- the closest original domestic/international scholarship relevant to this setting; verify bibliographic facts and label full text read, abstract only or bibliographic record only;
- what prior work already answers, what remains uncertain, and the bounded contribution this project could actually make; do not equate “related literature exists”, “a newer sample” or “no paper found” with a gap;
- construct definitions, units and observable measures; the proposed association or mechanism and at least one rival account or condition that could refute it;
- the actual evidence source, access conditions and a small obtained/parsed sample or equivalent primary-source check of the required fields, periods and unit; a data portal's metadata is not proof of a usable research sample;
- an inferential route appropriate to the question, material measurement/selection threats, scope and attainable resources;
- a proposed academic title whose wording matches those verified bounds, or `title: unresolved` with the missing prerequisite.

Do not require statistical significance, universal journal novelty or causal identification for a defensible descriptive master's project. Do require a nontrivial, argued contribution beyond repeating a textbook definition. A replication/comparison can qualify only with a justified purpose and an adequate design, not by renaming a two-series exercise.

## Gate and return

Explain the comparison and recommendation to the researcher, including its cost and alternatives; ask one consequential choice at a time. For a teaching role, record the authorised role's answer separately from a real student's or owner's decision. Never silently accept a title or add a moderator from an illustrative naming example.

Before `researchable`, obtain confirmation of the resolved research question and title state, verify all load-bearing literature/data premises, and read back the canonical summary with its sources. Retain negative feasibility results and unresolved uncertainty. If access, measurement, distinctness or scope is inadequate, return `repair`, `blocked` or `kill-candidate` with the exact failed premise and a concrete next check or alternative. A title-only edit cannot clear that gate.

## Teaching demonstration

Show what was missing before selection, how the receiving skill checked candidates, what evidence changed the recommendation, the puzzle/question/title distinction, and what allows advancement. Call unresolved footage “problem exploration”. Show real source/artifact screens; do not fabricate a successful data download, accepted title, screenshot or student confirmation.

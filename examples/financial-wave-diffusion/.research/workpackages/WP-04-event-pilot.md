# WP-04: Event-study pilot

- Status: blocked

## Decision or uncertainty reduced

Determine whether the predeclared mechanism signatures appear in one real shock window.

## End-to-end slice

Use an ex ante event and source market, estimate local-projection responses on the aligned panel, evaluate distance-arrival timing and spectral restrictions, and compare out-of-sample loss.

## Acceptance criteria

The analysis follows the predeclared event, graph, timing, estimand, baselines, and kill thresholds. All deviations are logged before results are interpreted.

## Falsifier and stop rule

Record a negative result if the telegraph restrictions fail. Do not select a new event after observing the outcome.

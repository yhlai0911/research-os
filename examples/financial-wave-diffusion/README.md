# Worked example: Financial Wave–Diffusion

This example shows how Research OS turns a physics-inspired finance idea into a narrower, falsifiable research design without claiming that markets literally obey a physical PDE.

The seed idea combined the Laplace, heat, and wave equations. The resolved design:

- removes Maxwell–Boltzmann and order-book data;
- compares graph diffusion with a damped-wave/telegraph alternative;
- treats the Laplace relation as a long-run boundary on a zero-sum cross-market field while modeling the global common mode separately;
- makes non-synchronous trading a central identification threat;
- refuses to claim a literature gap until a systematic boundary search is complete.

No empirical result is claimed. The example status is `conditional`: the design is coherent, but the literature boundary, aligned data panel, and model-separation pilot remain unfinished.

The example also includes a rendered partial academic research plan under `.research/plans/`. It preserves the blocked novelty claim, expresses milestones as evidence gates rather than invented dates, and contains no fabricated empirical results.

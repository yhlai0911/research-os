# From no topic to a manuscript with the research assistant

[Video,30-page PDF and research sources](https://github.com/yhlai0911/research-os/releases/tag/bank-term-deposit-tutorial-v1) · [Study index and checksums](../examples/bank-term-deposit-marketing/README.md) · [繁體中文](TUTORIAL_BANK.zh-TW.md)

Install Research-OS into your research project using the [quickstart](QUICKSTART.en.md), then begin:

```text
$research-assistant
I want to write a management/finance master's thesis, but I have no topic. Where should I start?
```

You do not need to adopt the film's eventual topic. Answer according to your own background, evidence and research decisions. The assistant selects/delegates; receiving skills perform the research.

|Current difficulty|Applicable skill and visible effect|Your next action|
|---|---|---|
|No topic|research-assistant hands off to research-1-grill, starting with experience and a puzzling observation|Answer the next necessary background/purpose question|
|Plausible idea, uncertain feasibility|data-feasibility, literature-landscape and applicable evidence skills check versions, fields and prior work|Inspect real sources and alternatives; repair or reject unsupported candidates|
|Question and methods do not yet align|research-2-brief and design skills align question, information, sample and method|Confirm the design; a proposal is not an empirical result|
|Concern about moving the goalposts|research-3-falsify defines counterevidence before research-4-tasks sets completion criteria|Check primary/secondary comparisons and stopping gates|
|Ready to execute one research slice|research-5-run produces verifiable outputs under the confirmed design|Open an actual response link; use sorting/charts only as needed, then return to the research question|
|Unsure results are ready to write|research-6-review checks the same version and requests repairs|Keep counterexamples and unresolved claims visible|
|Need a coherent manuscript|research-writing, citation-audit and applicable review skills connect evidence, prose and citations|Read real chapters and revisions; let the assistant check returned artifacts before routing onward|

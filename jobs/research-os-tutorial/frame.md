# Research OS Evidence Desk

The visual world is an editorial research desk fused with a quiet evidence-control room. Questions, claims, sources, decisions, and artifacts move through a visible pipeline; nothing appears magically complete.

## Do

- Use warm amber as the single attention color.
- Keep the canvas dark, ink-like, and slightly warm.
- Anchor labels and metadata to frame edges.
- Use large Traditional Chinese typography with sparse English system labels.
- Show state through real artifact names, status tags, evidence lines, and decision cards.
- Let the current frontier glow while downstream steps remain subdued.

## Do not

- Do not imitate the referenced YouTube thumbnail or reuse its imagery.
- Do not use cyan-purple gradients, generic glass cards, or a centered web-page stack.
- Do not present unverified claims as complete.
- Do not show fake empirical coefficients, p-values, citations, or dataset access.

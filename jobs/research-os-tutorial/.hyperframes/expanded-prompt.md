# Expanded production prompt

## Style block

- Canvas: 1920×1080, 16:9.
- Palette: ink `#0B1020`, paper `#F2F4E9`, amber `#FFCE57`, muted `#98A2B8`.
- Typography: `Noto Sans JP` for Traditional Chinese, `Space Mono` for metadata.
- Mood: editorial research desk meets evidence-control room.
- Concept angle: a vague topic becomes credible only by passing visible evidence gates.

## Rhythm

`HOOK — contrast — tutorial — DECISION — pipeline-build — output-breathe — CASE-STUDY — honest-hold`

## Global composition rules

- Use a monolithic composition because persistent background, evidence rail, and progress metadata continue across scenes.
- Every scene has background grid/noise, one oversized ghost term, a top metadata rail, a progress indicator, midground content, and foreground registration marks.
- Anchor content to zones and edges. Avoid centered floating card grids.
- Transitions are velocity-matched pushes, blur-throughs, and one geometric iris before the output scene.
- Scene content enters from its semantic direction: prompts type on, decisions branch, evidence gates fill left-to-right, outputs fan out.
- Captions use one concise Traditional Chinese phrase per scene, not word-for-word karaoke.

## Scene plan

### 1. Hook

The seed topic lands like an unverified specimen on a research desk. Three warning stamps—unknown data, unknown gap, unknown inference—lock around it. The final line converts the desk into a controlled pipeline.

### 2. Difference

Split the frame between a tall, polished but provenance-free draft and a compact modular skill chain. The draft looks impressive but has redacted evidence slots; the skill chain has explicit responsibilities.

### 3. Start

A terminal-like panel types a realistic `/develop-research` prompt. Labels point to topic, destination, audience, current state, and exclusions. A cursor clicks the command and the mission card locks.

### 4. Decision loop

A decision tree fills the right side. Only one current frontier glows amber; later branches remain muted. A human-decision card and a discoverable-fact card separate cleanly.

### 5. Evidence pipeline

A claim token moves through five gates: source, gap, identification, pilot, review. Each gate records a status rather than merely turning green. Reviewer pressure exposes the weakest load-bearing axis.

### 6. Outputs

A canonical Research Brief occupies the center. Three large destination artifacts move outward: research plan, proposal, paper blueprint. A manuscript stays locked until a results receipt appears.

### 7. Worked example

Four equation cards enter: wave, heat, Laplace, Maxwell–Boltzmann. The excluded Maxwell card is removed; Laplace becomes a boundary condition; wave and diffusion remain as competing models. The question rewrites itself into one discriminating sentence.

### 8. Honest close

Five evidence statuses align on a ruler: complete, partial, conditional, blocked, kill. The CTA is not “generate now” but “start with the first important question.”

## Negative prompt

- No copied YouTube frames, faces, logos, or thumbnail layout.
- No fake terminal output, citations, coefficients, p-values, or successful data access.
- No cyan-purple gradient, glassmorphism, generic SaaS cards, or dense tiny text.
- No animation driven by clocks, random values, hover, or network fetches.

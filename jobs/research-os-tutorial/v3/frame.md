# Research OS Evidence Desk — v3

Concept: a real research workbench where commands, decisions and evidence visibly move toward a reviewable contract.

- Canvas 1920×1080, 30 fps. Ink #0B1020, paper #F2F4E9, amber #FFCE57, muted #98A2B8. Preserve the original palette and quiet editorial register.
- Embed local Noto Sans CJK TC Regular/Black for complete Traditional Chinese coverage and Noto Sans Mono for exact commands. The v2 JP font is replaced deliberately to avoid Taiwanese glyph fallback. No render-time fonts or JavaScript downloads.
- Focal area: real terminal output, decision branch or research artifact. Secondary area: the consequence or verification status. Edge anchors: chapter, source commit, demonstration label, progress.
- Use geometric evidence paths, asymmetric document layouts and focused terminal zooms. No invented empirical coefficients, fabricated citations or research results.
- Label CLI replays as actual isolated verification; label authored Agent dialogue and conceptual diagrams as demonstrations.
- Keep commands verbatim, including --harness claude-code, --profile finance-social-science, $ in Codex and / in Claude. Clearly label isolated --target example paths.
- Opening comparison uses document/evidence geometry; closing returns to a visible next decision. No extra music; the new full narration carries the teaching.
- Motion vocabulary: svg-path-draw, viewport-change, discrete-text-sequence; explicit from/to states and a single paused timeline per scene. Use proof holds at zoom peaks and return to a readable wide frame.
